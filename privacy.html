Privacy Policy

TX for the Memories! · Effective November 20, 2026

In short: TX for the Memories! doesn't collect any information about you. Your Facebook data stays on your iPhone. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and doesn't send anything over the internet.

What the app does with your data

TX for the Memories! shows posts, photos and videos from your Facebook data export on the day they happened in earlier years. To do that, you choose the zip files from your export, and the app copies your posts, photos and videos into its own storage on your iPhone.

  • Your memories are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's private storage. They're never uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • The app only reads the files you choose. It can't see anything else in Files or iCloud Drive.
  • Your memories are encrypted by iOS whenever your iPhone is locked.
  • Your memories aren't included in iCloud Backup. If you set up a new iPhone, import your export again.
  • Your export can include other people's names, such as friends you tagged. Like the rest of your memories, they stay on your iPhone.

Information we collect

None. We don't collect, store, sell or share any personal information. We have no servers that receive data from the app, and the app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code. The app doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Internet connections

The app doesn't connect to the internet. Some things you do in it involve other apps or Apple services, which follow their own privacy policies:

  • If your export's zip files are in iCloud Drive, iOS downloads them from iCloud when you choose them.
  • Tapping a link in a post opens it in Safari, and tapping a place opens it in Apple Maps.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks for permission to show notifications. The reminders are scheduled on your iPhone and don't use any server. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in the iPhone Settings app.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. Apple collects and provides this information under Apple's privacy policy, and it doesn't identify you. You can turn it off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Deleting your data

To delete everything the app has stored, open the app's Settings and tap Delete Imported Data, or delete the app from your iPhone. Because we never receive your data, there's nothing to delete on our side.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Facebook

TX for the Memories! isn't affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Meta Platforms, Inc. Facebook is a trademark of Meta Platforms, Inc. The app reads the data export Facebook provides to you; it doesn't connect to Facebook or use your Facebook account.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
