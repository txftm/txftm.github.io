TX for the Memories!

Your Facebook memories, without Facebook.

See your posts, photos and videos from this day in earlier years, from your own Facebook data export. Everything stays on your iPhone.

  • Imports the zip files from Facebook's "Download your information" export.
  • Shows each day's memories by year, with photos, videos, places and links.
  • Optional daily reminder.
  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no internet connection needed.
