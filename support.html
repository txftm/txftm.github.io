Common questions

How do I get my Facebook export?
On facebook.com, go to Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information. Choose JSON format, All time, and High media quality. Facebook emails you when it's ready, often as several zip files. Save them to iCloud Drive, or download them on your iPhone in Safari. Don't unzip them.
Some photos are missing or gray.
Facebook often splits an export into several zip files, with posts in one and photos in another. Import again and choose all of the zip files at once. Importing again only adds what's missing. The app's Settings shows how many photos and videos are missing.
The app says the zip files don't contain Facebook posts.
The export was probably made in HTML format. Request a new one in JSON format.
The app says there isn't enough space.
Your photos and videos need room on your iPhone. Free up the amount the message shows, then import again.
Do I need to keep the zip files?
Not for the app to work: it keeps its own copy. Your memories aren't included in iCloud Backup, though, so keep the zip files in iCloud Drive if you'd like to set up a new iPhone later.
The daily reminder doesn't appear.
Check that notifications are allowed in the iPhone Settings app → Notifications → TXFTM. Open the app at least once every couple of months to keep the reminders scheduled.
How do I delete my data?
In the app's Settings, tap Delete Imported Data, or delete the app. Your data is only on your iPhone, so that removes it completely. See the privacy policy.
